# Privacy Policy — Current

**Last updated: July 25, 2026**

Current is a music app made by clectric.audio ("we," "us"). We designed Current to respect your privacy. **We do not collect personal information, we do not track you, we do not use advertising, and we do not share your data with third parties.** There are no third‑party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

This policy explains the small amount of data the app handles and why.

## The short version

- **No account, no sign‑up, no login.** You can use Current without giving us anything.
- **No tracking, no ads, no third‑party analytics.**
- **Your settings sync through *your own* iCloud** — we can't see them.
- **The only information we ever receive is a diagnostic report you choose to email us** —

## iCloud sync (your own data, your own iCloud)

If you are signed in to iCloud, Current uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to sync **your own in‑app settings** across your devices — things like which sound packs you've pulled into your crate, your per‑pack tweaks, and any custom packs you create. This information is stored in **your private iCloud account**. It is not sent to clectric.audio, and **we have no ability to see or access it.** Its handling is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). If you don't use iCloud, this data simply stays on your device.

## Feedback you choose to send us

Current has an optional "Send Feedback" screen. If you use it, the app opens **your own email app** with a pre‑filled message that includes a technical diagnostic snapshot to help us understand and fix problems. That snapshot contains only non‑personal, device‑ and app‑related information, such as:

- app version and build, and basic usage counts (e.g. number of sessions, minutes played);
- your device model and iOS version, and your device locale/region setting;
- audio settings and status (audio output, buffer size, sample rate, engine state);
- device condition relevant to audio performance (thermal state, low‑power mode, free disk space, memory use, whether VoiceOver is on);
- the current sound pack and musical settings (tempo, key, scale, and similar).

**It contains no contacts, no location, no photos, no files, no microphone data, and no message content beyond what you write.** You can review and edit the message before sending, and nothing is sent unless **you** tap send in your email app. If you email us, we receive your message, the diagnostic snapshot, and your email address, and we use them **only** to respond to you and improve the app. We do not sell this information or share it with anyone, and we don't use it to build a profile of you or to track you.

## Data required for basic app function

Like most apps, Current stores your preferences on your device (using the system's standard preferences storage) and reads basic device information such as available disk space and system uptime. This is used only to make the app work and to populate the optional diagnostic report described above. It stays on your device unless you choose to email us.

## Children

Current is a music‑making tool suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children, and the app requires none.

## Data retention and your choices

We keep feedback emails only as long as needed to provide support and improve the app, and you may ask us to delete any email you've sent us. Your iCloud data is managed by you in your iCloud account and can be removed there at any time.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and post the new version at this URL. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
