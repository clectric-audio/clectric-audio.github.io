# Current — Support

**Current** is a synthesis‑based groovebox by clectric.audio: a playable instrument for learning, discovering, and performing music. This page is the support contact for the app.

## Get help

- **Email:** [support@clectric.audio](mailto:support@clectric.audio)
- **In the app:** open **Settings ▸ Send Feedback**, choose a category (Bug Report, Feature Request, Sound Design, Thank You, or Other), and tap **Write Email**. This attaches a short technical diagnostic that helps us reproduce issues faster. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

We aim to reply within 2 business days.

## Quick answers

- **How do I get more sound packs?** Open **Crates** to browse packs. Pulling a pack that isn't installed yet downloads its sounds on demand (Wi‑Fi recommended). Packs you've pulled appear in the pack picker, ready to play.
- **Do my settings sync between devices?** Yes — if you're signed in to iCloud, your crate, per‑pack tweaks, and custom packs sync through your own iCloud account automatically.
- **The audio crackled or the device got warm.** Close other audio apps and try again; if it persists, send us feedback from **Settings ▸ Send Feedback** so we get the diagnostics.

## Privacy

See our [Privacy Policy](https://clectric.audio/privacy). Short version: no accounts, no tracking, no ads; your settings sync via your own iCloud; we only ever receive a diagnostic report if you choose to email it to us.
